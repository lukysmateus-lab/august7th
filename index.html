Helpful tips when two or more adjectives are used before a noun:

1. general before specific:

A Brazilian famous model.

A famous Brazilian model.

2. opinion before description:

An electric cool bicycle.

A cool electric bicycle.

GERUNDS

We use a gerund in the following situations:

Situation Example
1. As the subject of a sentence Winning the lottery is a dream of mine.
2. Following a preposition The student apologised for being late.
3. Following certain verbs I miss chatting with Vinnie.

These verbs are usually followed by verbs in the gerund form:
